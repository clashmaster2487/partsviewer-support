Privacy policy

PartsViewer does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

PartsViewer stores parts information in a local SQLite database on the user's device. This information is not automatically sent to the developer or to third parties.

PartsViewer does not require an online account or cloud service for its core functionality.

If you have questions about the privacy practices of PartsViewer, please contact partsviewersupport@gmail.com.
